Quantum Stock Picker

Choose a few stocks out of many, with high profit and low risk, by asking a quantum circuit (QAOA, built in Qiskit) and checking its answer against trying every combination.

What changes as the problem grows

Same QAOA depth and a similar optimiser budget at every size. Each bigger problem has twice as many possibilities for every added stock.

How it works

  1. Encode. One qubit per stock: |1> means picked, |0> means left out.
  2. Score. Cost = risk − profit + a penalty if we do not pick exactly k stocks. This becomes a Hamiltonian made of Z and ZZ terms.
  3. Solve. QAOA alternates a cost layer and a mixer layer, p times. A classical optimiser (COBYLA) tunes the layer angles to lower the average cost.
  4. Read out. Measure the circuit 2048 times and keep the cheapest valid portfolio.
  5. Check. Compare with the exhaustive search over all 2n portfolios.
Data are synthetic (seeded sector-factor model), so results demonstrate the method, not investment advice. Simulations run on a classical computer, not real quantum hardware.
